Chance is 0.0250%. 31.746 (31.000–32.340)% of pairs share at least one canonical 31-mer between their two mates, which is why the disjoint row is the honest one: an overlapping pair's two mates contain the same bases, so their coverage profiles agree by construction rather than by biology. That test is conservative — a shared 31-mer can also come from repeat content — so it over-assigns to the overlapping half and leaves the disjoint half clean.
