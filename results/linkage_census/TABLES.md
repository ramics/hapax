## Without the witnesses that overlap their own mate

About a third of this library's pairs overlap their mate, so for those the "other" mate is partly the same sequence as the matching one. Dropping every witness whose two mates both match the query removes that path entirely, at the cost of moving queries into `no_witnesses`.
